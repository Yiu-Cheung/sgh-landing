## 1. Open Settings → Credentials

Click **Smart Gallery Hub** (top-left) → **Settings**.

![Smart Gallery Hub main menu](/tutorials/credential-setup/01-open-settings-menu.png)

In the sidebar, click **Credentials**.

## 2. Adobe Stock

![Credentials panel with Adobe Stock fields](/tutorials/credential-setup/02-credentials-panel.png)

- Toggle **Adobe Stock** on.
- Fill **Portal Email + Password** → click **Test login session**.
- Fill **SFTP Username + Password** → click **Test SFTP Login**.

Two green checks = done.

## 3. Shutterstock

- Toggle **ShutterStock** on.
- Fill **Username / Email + Password** → click **Test login session**.

## 4. Getty / iStock

- Toggle **Getty** on.
- Fill **ESP Username + Password** → click **Test login session**.

## 5. Submit

Back in the workspace, tick your images → **Operate → Adobestock / Shutterstock / Getty**.
